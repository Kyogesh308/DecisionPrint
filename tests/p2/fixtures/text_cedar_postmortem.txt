Incident Report: Cedar Storage Outage
Date: 2025-11-03

On November 2nd, the Cedar project experienced a major storage outage when the primary database cluster ran out of space.
Because automated backups were disabled to save money in Q1, we had to rely on manual dumps which were 48 hours old.
The postmortem explicitly identifies the decision to disable automated backups as the primary contributing factor to the extended recovery window.
As a result of this, recovery took 14 hours due to absence of automated backups.
We lost significant customer trust and missed our quarterly SLA targets.
